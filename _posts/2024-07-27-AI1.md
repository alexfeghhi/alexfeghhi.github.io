<h1>Intro</h1>

Over the past year, I've applied to a variety of grad schools and job postings. <em>I've been rejected from all of them.</em> What's been particularly troubling is the way I've been rejected; no human contact, no interview, just automated emails.

<img src="https://i.imgur.com/Qqo5Z5o.png" alt="My MIT rejection" />

The jobs I applied to were tech-adjacent; software engineering, data science, data engineering, machine learning, etc. The graduate schools were AI related; PhD programs in Cognitive Science, Master's programs in Computer Science.

Now, I could have done things differently; be less selective: applying to less prestigious schools, apply to jobs outside of my hometown, the Bay Area. But, to be honest, I never expected to be rejected across the board, and I think my assumptions were reasonable.

<h1>My Background</h1>

Straight out of high school, I got into USC with most of the tuition covered. Ultimately, I bounced around several schools, such as Foothill Communtity College, Skyline College(for wrestling) and finally graduated from UC Santa Cruz(UCSC for short) in five years. My acheivements, over that time period, were as follows:
<ul>
  <li>Completed a year-long internship at NASA HECC(High End Computing Center), working on a machine learning project for sunspot prediction</li>
  <li>Working in various research labs at UCSC, such as a computational neuroscience lab, a cognitive science lab, and an explanable artificial intelligence lab</li>
  <li>Graduated from UCSC with a Major in Cognitive Science and a Minor in Computer Science</li>
</ul>

These credentials might seem impressive to some, but I personally think they're that great.
